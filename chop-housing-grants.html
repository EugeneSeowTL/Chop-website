Family Grant and Proximity Housing Grant apply to resale purchases only. Family Grant assumes a $14,000 income ceiling (up to $80,000) — extended families buying with parents get a higher $21,000 ceiling, not modelled here. Second-timers typically receive a reduced amount (e.g. Half-Housing Grant) not calculated in this simplified version. Doesn't account for the 30-month private property disposal rule, non-citizen spouse schemes, or Step-Up Grant eligibility. Confirm your real entitlement via your HFE letter or HDB directly.
