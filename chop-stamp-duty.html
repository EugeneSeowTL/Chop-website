BSD tier breakdown
BSD applies to every buyer regardless of residency, on a progressive scale: 1% on the first $180,000, 2% on the next $180,000, 3% on the next $640,000, 4% on the next $500,000, 5% on the next $1.5M, and 6% above $3M. ABSD is layered on top and depends on your residency status and how many residential properties you already own — Singapore Citizens pay 0% on a first property, PRs pay 5%, foreigners pay a flat 60%, entities 65%. Nationals of Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland, and the USA are treated as Citizens under free trade agreements (not reflected in this simple calculator — check IRAS if this applies to you). This tool doesn't account for joint-purchase structuring, married-couple remissions, or trust arrangements, which can change your actual figure — always confirm with IRAS or a conveyancing lawyer before committing to a purchase.
